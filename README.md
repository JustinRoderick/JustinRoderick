### How to play

- I play one color, everyone else plays the other
- Turns alternate: I move, then any visitor makes the next move for the visitors' side, then it's my turn again
- Play on a desktop browser. On a phone the board is scaled down and clicks land on the wrong squares
- When a game ends, click the board to start the next one. Starting colors are random
